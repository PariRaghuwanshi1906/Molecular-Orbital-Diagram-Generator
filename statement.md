# Molecular Orbital Diagram Generator

## Problem Statement
Understanding molecular orbital theory and visualizing molecular orbitals is a crucial part of learning chemistry, especially for diatomic molecules. Traditional methods often involve drawing diagrams manually, which can be time-consuming, error-prone, and difficult for beginners to interpret. There is a need for an automated, intuitive tool that can generate accurate molecular orbital diagrams with clear visualization of bonding, antibonding, and nonbonding orbitals, making the learning process easier and more interactive.

## Scope of the Project
The Molecular Orbital Diagram Generator aims to simplify the visualization of molecular orbitals for educational and research purposes. The project covers:
- Generation of molecular orbital diagrams for diatomic molecules.
- Display of bonding, antibonding, and nonbonding orbitals along with electron occupancy.
- Options for user input, including custom electron configurations or default atomic data.
- Exporting and saving diagrams for further reference or study.
- Enhancing conceptual understanding of molecular orbital theory and electron distribution.

## Target Users
The primary target users of this project are:
- **Students** studying chemistry at the high school or undergraduate level.
- **Teachers and educators** who need a quick and clear way to demonstrate molecular orbital concepts.
- **Researchers** and chemistry enthusiasts seeking accurate visualizations of molecular orbitals for analysis or presentation.

## High-Level Features
The Molecular Orbital Diagram Generator includes the following key features:
1. **User-Friendly Interface:** Easy navigation with a main menu and clear options for generating, viewing, and exporting diagrams.
2. **Molecule Selection:** Ability to select from a list of diatomic molecules and input atomic numbers for precise calculations.
3. **Custom Electron Configurations:** Users can specify electron counts or use default configurations based on molecular orbital rules.
4. **Diagram Generation:** Automatic creation of molecular orbital diagrams showing bonding, antibonding, and nonbonding orbitals with electrons distributed according to Aufbau principle and Hund’s rule.
5. **Export and Save Options:** Diagrams can be saved or exported as images for study, assignments, or presentations.
6. **Educational Aid:** Helps users understand molecular stability, electron configuration, and orbital energy levels through clear visualization.
